# Photo

Workshop and product photography with `radius-lg` corners and a caption under it: an optional serif `<em>` phrase in walnut, then plain `small` text in `ink-muted`.

- Provide: a real photo with a descriptive `alt`, its aspect ratio, and a short caption.
- `vw-photo-grid` pairs a wide 4:3 photo with a square one, bottoms aligned.
- Prefer natural, warm light and visible wood grain. No stock photos, no AI renders on the live site.
