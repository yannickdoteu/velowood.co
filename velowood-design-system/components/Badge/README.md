# Badge

Small pills for facts and certifications: `vw-badge` (honey-soft ground with a tiny ring dot), `vw-badge--cert` (moss dot, for FSC and PEFC), `vw-badge--outline` (hairline only).

- Provide: two to four words of text in a `vw-badges` list.
- The moss dot is reserved for certifications: it is where the green lives.
- Facts only, never marketing adjectives.
