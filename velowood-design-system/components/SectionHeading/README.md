# SectionHeading

The opener of every section: `vw-eyebrow` (uppercase label with a small moss dot), `vw-heading` (`heading-1`, or `vw-heading--display` for big section openers) and an optional `vw-lead` in `ink-muted`.

- Provide: a one- or two-word eyebrow, a heading under ~8 words, and at most one lead paragraph.
- On `vw-section--walnut` the eyebrow turns honey and the lead `on-walnut-muted` automatically.
