# Photography

The brand's photos come from the workshop in Greece (the former Coco-Mat bike workshop, now velowood).

- `craft-templates.jpg` — a maker choosing a bent frame part in front of the template wall. The hero photo.
- `workshop-fitting.jpg` and `workshop-fitting-graded.jpg` — two makers checking frame parts on the floor, natural and colour-graded. Prefer the graded one.
- `craft-sanding.jpg` — a maker shaping a frame part, soft background. Square crops.
- `concept-frame-render.jpg` — AI concept render of a frame with the wordmark. Mood reference only: do not publish it as a product photo.

Style: warm natural light, visible grain, hands at work, real people. Crop with `radius-lg` corners. No stock imagery, no filters that cool the wood down.
